# Customer email drafts with review

Synthetic example. The request, vendor, reviewer, and human decision below are fictional.

## Request

The customer support team proposes a DraftDesk AI agent that drafts replies to ordinary service questions. Five named support staff operate it; customers receive only replies that a staff member has checked and sent. Inputs come from company-owned support tickets and include customer names, email addresses, and order references. Payment details, credentials, health information, and legally privileged material are excluded.

The agent reads selected tickets and produces draft text in a restricted queue. It cannot send email, change an order, issue refunds, or make eligibility decisions. Staff check facts, tone, and recipient details before sending. The fictional enterprise vendor is expressly approved for these inputs, does not train on tickets, and deletes processing copies within 24 hours. Ticket access follows existing support permissions. Drafts are deleted after 30 days. Company ownership and stated customer permissions cover this processing and use. Supervisors sample replies weekly and can disable the integration.

## Decision

**Approved with conditions** by a fictional human reviewer on 2026-09-10, under the illustrative starter rubric, version 0.1.

## Why

Personal data and externally delivered replies create meaningful confidentiality, vendor, and reliability exposure (D1, D2, D5). Mandatory review, restricted integration privileges, and deletion controls make the risk bounded (D3, D4). B1 does not apply to the expressly suitable vendor; B2 does not apply to drafts sent only after human approval.

## Conditions

- Enforce exclusions for sensitive ticket content before processing.
- Keep the sending permission unavailable to the agent.
- Review all drafts and track factual errors and accidental disclosures.
- Confirm the stated retention and no-training controls before the pilot starts.

This is a fictional recorded human outcome. Its conditions are not evidence that a new request has already implemented these controls.
