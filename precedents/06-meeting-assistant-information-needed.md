# Meeting assistant with unresolved handling terms

Synthetic example. The request, vendor, reviewer, and human decision below are fictional.

## Request

The operations team proposes MinuteMate, an AI-enabled meeting recorder, to prepare internal transcripts, summaries, and proposed action items. Named team members would operate it and review summaries before sharing them within the team. It would not send messages or carry out action items. Recordings may include voices, names, routine internal discussions, and confidential plans. Participants and team operations could be affected by disclosure or inaccurate summaries.

The company owns its meeting documents, but recording permissions have not been established. Tool approval for confidential recordings, vendor training, external sharing, vendor retention, local deletion periods, and access restrictions are all Unknown. Human review is planned, but no other controls or monitoring are defined.

## Decision

**More information requested** by a fictional human reviewer on 2026-09-22, under the illustrative starter rubric, version 0.1. No approval was recorded.

## Why

The description explains the productivity goal but leaves material privacy, security, permissions, and retention facts unresolved (D1, D2, D6). B1 remains unknown rather than assumed absent. Human review of summaries does not supply the missing recording or vendor permissions. Unknown handling facts cannot be scored as low risk.

## Information requested

- Recording and processing permissions, including how participants are informed.
- Data classification and tool suitability for the stated inputs.
- Named access boundaries, vendor training and sharing terms, and retention controls.
- A clear method for checking summaries and handling errors.

This file is a useful precedent for an incomplete submission, not an approval precedent.
